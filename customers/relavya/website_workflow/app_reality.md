## What the app does (owner-stated)
- Set a conference, expo or meetup as the ACTIVE EVENT; people saved are filed under it.
- Save a person with a name only; company, number and card photo are optional and can be added later.
- Record one line on what you talked about and a next step.
- TODAY view: who is overdue, who is due today, whom you met this week.
- Writes a follow-up from the saved memory for WhatsApp, LinkedIn or email; message kinds: first follow-up, thanks, resource, congratulations, meeting request, introduction. Templates work offline.
- Optional "Improve with AI" rewrite (needs a connection).
- Opens the message in the user's OWN WhatsApp / LinkedIn / email. Never sends anything itself.
- Asks "Did you send it?" on return; only "Yes, sent" marks the person as followed up.
- Reminders for a message or a call, with quiet hours and the contact's local time.
- Occasion greetings (Diwali, Pongal, New Year, birthdays, "a year since we met").
- Notes on achievements, business, family, dreams and ideas feed later messages.
- A shareable card (Professional / Founder pitch / Casual) with a QR code; recipients need no install.
- Works offline for saving people, notes and message templates. Reading a card photo and the AI rewrite need a connection.
- Installable web app (Android via Chrome install; iOS via Safari "Add to Home Screen"); also works in a desktop browser.

## Privacy and data (owner-stated)
- Data is private to the account; not shared between accounts, not sold, not used to train models.
- The public card shows only the fields the user publishes.
- Export as JSON or CSV, and account deletion, from Settings.

## Commercial (owner-stated)
