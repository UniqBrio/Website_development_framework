Privacy & data

Your notebook, not ours.

The people you meet trusted you with their details. Relavya is built so that trust stays with you.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

What is Relavya?

Relavya is a follow-up app for people who meet people at events. You save someone with a name and one line on what you talked about; afterwards Relavya shows who is due for a follow-up and opens a ready, personal message in your own WhatsApp, LinkedIn or email.

Does Relavya send messages for me?

No. Relavya never messages anyone. The follow-up opens in your own WhatsApp, LinkedIn or email, and you press send. When you come back, Relavya asks “Did you send it?” — only your “Yes, sent” marks the person as followed up.

Do I need to download it from an app store?

No. Relavya is a web app you install from your browser. Tap “Download for Android” or “Download for iOS” on this site: on Android, Chrome offers an Install button (or use “Install app” in the browser menu); on iPhone and iPad, open it in Safari, tap Share and then “Add to Home Screen”. It also works in a desktop browser.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, for what matters at a venue. Saving people, adding notes and writing message templates all work offline. Reading a card photo and the optional AI rewrite need a connection.

What happens to the contact details I save?

They are private to your account. Relavya does not share contact data between accounts, sell it, or use it to train models. Your public card shows only the fields you choose to publish.

How much does it cost?

Creating an account is free. Relavya is in private beta, and there is no paid plan today.
