**Subject:** New website for our practice

Hi,

We are **Ashvattha Tax & Compliance**, a small tax and accounting practice in
**Baner, Pune**. Two partners — I am a Chartered Accountant, my partner looks after
GST work — plus three staff. We started in **2014**.

Right now we only have a Google Business listing and a Facebook page. Most new
clients find us by searching things like "GST consultant in Pune" or "ITR filing
near me", usually on their phones. We want a proper website so we show up on
Google and people can contact us.

**What we do:**

1. **GST registration and GST return filing** (monthly / quarterly) — for traders,
   shops, service businesses and online sellers who need GSTIN, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B
   and annual returns done correctly and on time.
2. **Income tax return filing** — for salaried people, freelancers and small
   business owners. We also handle notices from the income tax department.
3. **Bookkeeping and accounting** — monthly books on Tally or Zoho Books for small
   businesses that don't want a full-time accountant.
4. **Company and LLP registration** and yearly ROC compliance.
5. **TDS returns.**

**Who we want:** small business owners, startup founders, freelancers, and salaried
people who find tax confusing. Mostly Pune and nearby (Pimpri-Chinchwad, Hinjewadi,
Wakad), but we do GST and ITR work online for people anywhere in Maharashtra.

**What we want the website to do:**
- Get more enquiries — calls, WhatsApp or email.
- Rank on Google for our services in Pune.
- Look trustworthy and professional, modern but not flashy. Not boring corporate
  either. People should feel they can just ask us a question.
- A separate page for each service would be good for Google, I've been told.
- An FAQ, because we get asked the same things every week.
- A simple contact form that emails us. A WhatsApp button. A map to the office.
- Maybe a blog later, not now.

**Pages:** Home, Services (and one page per service), About us, FAQ, Contact.

**Proof:** We have 60+ regular clients and a 4.9 rating on Google from about 80
reviews. Clients are happy to give testimonials if we ask. I am an ICAI member.

**Prices:** ITR filing starts at ₹999 and GST returns from ₹1,500 a month, but it
depends on the client, so maybe don't put prices? You decide what is best.

**Branding:** We have a logo — dark blue text with a small leaf. I'll send the file.
No fixed colours apart from that. We have some photos of the office taken on a phone,
not great quality.

**Domain:** We bought **ashvattha-tax.example**.

**Contact:** Email is **hello@ashvattha-tax.example**. I'll send the WhatsApp number
and the full office address with the logo. We meet clients at the office by
appointment.

**Language:** English is fine. Maybe Marathi later.

**Timeline:** Ideally before the tax filing rush in July.

**Something we like:** ClearTax's website feels modern and easy.

Thanks,
Managing Partner
