Executive Summary
UniqBrio has a genuinely compelling product for a real, underserved market — Indian arts and sports academy owners drowning in admin work. The homepage follows most of the blueprint's scroll sequence, and the pricing page (transparent, three-tier, with a monthly/annual toggle) is one of the strongest sections on the site. The ROI Calculator is a standout lead magnet rare in this category. However, the site scores 56/100 primarily because it leaks revenue in five structural areas: a fragmented hero headline that cannot achieve 5-second clarity; a critically thin social proof layer (two testimonials, one with a stock/placeholder headshot, zero third-party badges); a complete absence of an About page, case studies, and blog; no analytics or tracking infrastructure confirmed; and missing legal/compliance elements including a cookie consent banner. The most impactful single fix is consolidating the hero into one outcome-focused H1 and building a credible social proof stack. At 56/100, UniqBrio should pause paid traffic investment and execute the 14-day sprint in this report before scaling acquisition.

Section 07 Design Elements 6 / 10
✓ Strengths
  • Restrained color palette (brand blue/navy with white backgrounds and green accent for success states) — cohesive and readable for the target non-technical audience.
  • The Before/After problem section uses a clean card layout with clear visual contrast — communicates transformation efficiently. The ROI Calculator is an interactive design standout rare in this category.
  • Strong visual hierarchy across sections with modern, readable fonts — the overall aesthetic is appropriate for a professional mobile-first SaaS product targeting Indian small business owners.
→ Improvements
  • Verified body font size must be ≥ 16px: Multiple audit sources flagged that Indian SaaS sites frequently use 14px body text. Smaller type prevents iOS auto-zoom on inputs, reduces readability on small screens, and fails WCAG guidelines. Audit all body text — set a minimum of 16px globally.
  • Justified text in Vision/Mission section: The blueprint explicitly warns against justified text — it creates uneven word spacing that reduces reading speed, especially on mobile. Switch all body text to left-aligned.
  • No dark mode support: The blueprint flags dark mode as "increasingly expected in SaaS 2026," and Indian mobile users frequently use dark mode. Add prefers-color-scheme: dark CSS media query at minimum — even a simple dark surface implementation signals technical care.
  • Add subtle micro-animations: Button hover states (scale + shadow), scroll-reveal fade-ins on section entry, and loading feedback on form submission. These make the experience feel premium without adding visual noise.
  • Whitespace audit: Ensure 80–120px vertical padding between all major sections for visual breathing room — particularly between the features grid and the pricing section.
